Founder Bio

A multidisciplinary creative technology founder.

Chibueze Henry Victor is a Nigerian creative professional, creative technologist and entrepreneur whose work brings together filmmaking, artificial intelligence, digital products, technology and education.

His creative journey began through visual storytelling, video production, cinematography, editing, digital content and brand communication. Over time, his interests expanded beyond traditional media production into artificial intelligence, automation, software development and digital product creation.

He has worked as a cinematographer, video editor, creative director, AI animator, digital product builder, trainer and team leader. Through Prime Creative, he is building a multidisciplinary creative technology company focused on creating compelling visual experiences, building digital products and teaching practical creative technology skills.

Founder Quote

"I don't want Prime Creative to simply be known for making beautiful things. I want us to build things that work, create experiences people remember, and teach people how to create opportunities for themselves."

Leadership Philosophy

Leading through building.

Practical execution, continuous learning, responsibility and creative independence.

01

Lead by doing

Understand the work, participate in solving difficult problems and demonstrate the standard expected from the team.

02

Build people, not dependence

Develop capable people who can make decisions, solve problems and produce excellent work without constant supervision.

03

Keep learning

Creative and technology industries evolve quickly, so teams must continuously learn new tools, workflows and ways of thinking.

04

Combine creativity with structure

Creativity produces ideas, but systems turn those ideas into repeatable results.

05

Execution matters

Ideas become valuable when they are built, tested, improved and delivered.

06

Create opportunities

Creative businesses should produce projects and create opportunities for others to develop skills, careers and ideas.
