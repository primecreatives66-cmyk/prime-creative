Food Technology / Digital Product

Chowdown

A branded food ordering platform designed for individual food vendors.

Role

Product Design & Development

Product Type

Vendor-owned food ordering platform.

Project Overview

Chowdown is a food ordering platform designed specifically for individual food vendors rather than a multi-vendor marketplace.

Platform Direction

The system allows a food business to operate its own branded digital ordering experience where customers can browse meals, place orders, track orders and interact with the vendor.

Business Features

  • Branded customer ordering flow
  • Meal browsing and order placement
  • Order tracking direction
  • Menu management
  • Customer and delivery information structure
  • Operational settings for the food business
